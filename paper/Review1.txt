The authors report the numbers of semirings up to order 7 or 8 depending on additional constraint variants, counting one element from each isomorphic and anti isomorphic equivalence class. The calculations were performed using the GAP smallsemi library, and identified a small error in the output of the ALG package. Preparation for the calculations is greatly enhanced by the use of the key theorem that links isomorphism to membership of selected double cosets. This approach rules out the need for the searching and symmetry breaking used to enumerate semigroups up to order 10, and allows pre-computed values to be used in conjunction with GAP graph automorphism calculations to enumerate the representative double coset members required by the theorem. In summary, the authors have devised a useful result to simplify calculations and then used GAP code to produce the tables of enumeration results. 

I recommend acceptance.
